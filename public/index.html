Questions

Why Google sign-in only?

It keeps sign-up to one click and means we never store a password. We only receive your name, email address and profile picture.

Where are my CVs stored?

In your CVCraft account, saved automatically as you type. You can also download any CV as a file and open it later.

Will the PDF pass applicant tracking systems?

Yes. The PDF keeps real, selectable text, and the ATS Simple layout avoids columns and graphics that confuse parsers.

Can I keep more than one CV?

Yes, up to 25. Make one per region or per job, then duplicate and adjust.
